INTERPRETATION
1. The 350-record manual audit is now complete.
2. Technical duplicate/paratext exclusions are separated from substantive exclusions.
3. Candidate exclusions are NOT yet final PRISMA exclusions; they require verification because calibration performance from a finite audit sample does not prove zero false exclusions in the full corpus.
4. Full-text advancement is intentionally sensitive: false positives can be removed at full-text review, whereas false negative title/abstract exclusions would threaten review validity.
5. The broad OpenAlex corpus remains the knowledge-development/bibliometric corpus even when records do not enter the primary evidence synthesis.

DISCLOSURE
This screening workflow uses AI/rule-assisted prioritisation and a calibrated text model. Any manuscript should describe this assistance transparently and should not state that the author manually screened every record unless the author independently verifies those decisions.
