LOCKED SYSTEMATIC REVIEW PROTOCOL — VERSION 1.0
Locked: 12 September 2026

TITLE
Health Financing for Universal Health Coverage in Low- and Lower-Middle-Income Countries: Potential and Challenges

PRIMARY DESIGN
Systematic review and structured evidence synthesis. Bibliometric/science-mapping analyses are secondary methods used to characterise knowledge development, not the primary study design.

REVIEW QUESTION
How has knowledge on health-financing approaches for UHC developed in low- and lower-middle-income countries; what evidence exists regarding their potential and challenges; under what contextual conditions do the approaches perform well or poorly; and where do important evidence gaps remain?

OBJECTIVES
1. Systematically identify evidence on health-financing approaches relevant to UHC in low- and lower-middle-income countries.
2. Synthesize the potential, challenges, implementation conditions and UHC implications of each financing approach.
3. Examine knowledge development over time, geography, institutions, collaboration and themes using bibliometric/science-mapping methods applied to the broad OpenAlex retrieval corpus.
4. Map evidence gaps by financing approach, country/context, population, UHC outcome and evidence strength.

INFORMATION SOURCE AND SEARCH FRAME
Primary source: OpenAlex. Retrieval window: 1990–2026. Work types at retrieval: article or review. Retracted works were excluded by the query. Geography uses the supplied World Bank FY2027 fixed cohort of 25 low-income and 47 lower-middle-income economies. The exact OpenAlex OQL query is preserved separately.

ELIGIBILITY — INCLUDE
Include studies whose title/abstract/full text substantively evaluates or analyses at least one health-financing mechanism/function relevant to UHC in one or more target economies (or multi-country analyses with relevant/extractable target-country evidence). Eligible financing domains include health-financing reform; tax/general-revenue financing; social/national/community-based health insurance or prepayment; pooling; strategic purchasing/provider payment; results/performance-based financing; user fees/cost sharing; out-of-pocket expenditure/financial protection; external/donor financing; and mixed arrangements.

Eligible UHC outcomes include population/service coverage or entitlement; access/utilization; financial protection; equity; efficiency/value; quality where linked to financing; revenue/resource mobilization; fiscal/financial sustainability; pooling/fragmentation; purchasing/provider incentives; implementation/governance; and documented enabling or limiting conditions.

ELIGIBILITY — EXCLUDE
Exclude duplicates; paratext/corrections without substantive evidence; papers not substantively about health financing; purely clinical/biomedical studies; cost-of-illness or cost-effectiveness studies with no health-financing mechanism or system-policy implication; hospital/accounting finance without UHC relevance; studies outside target economies without relevant target-country evidence; and records where financing is incidental. Disease-specific financing studies are eligible only when they evaluate a financing mechanism/function with transferable UHC relevance.

STUDY DESIGNS
Empirical quantitative, qualitative, mixed-methods, implementation/policy evaluations, natural/quasi-experiments, observational analyses and evidence syntheses may be included. Conceptual/commentary papers are excluded from the primary empirical synthesis unless they contribute a clearly defined system/policy framework; if retained for contextual synthesis they are labelled separately and assigned lower inferential weight.

SCREENING PROCESS
Stage 0: technical deduplication using valid DOI; otherwise normalized title plus year. Invalid/blank DOI strings are ignored for deduplication. The metadata-complete record is retained.
Stage 1: title/abstract screening against these locked criteria. The existing high-specificity OpenAlex financing screen is used only to PRIORITISE records; it is not a final inclusion decision. All other unique substantive records remain in manual-review Priority B rather than being automatically excluded.
Stage 2: full-text assessment with one primary exclusion reason for each excluded report.
Stage 3: structured extraction and design-specific critical appraisal.

AUTOMATION AND AUDIT
No record is finally excluded solely because of the automated triage. A fixed-seed audit/calibration sample is supplied. Any later automation rule must be versioned and audited before use for final exclusions.

CRITICAL APPRAISAL
Use design-specific JBI critical-appraisal tools where applicable. Evidence syntheses may additionally use an appropriate review-appraisal instrument such as AMSTAR 2 when applicable. Do not collapse heterogeneous designs into one numeric quality score.

SYNTHESIS
Primary synthesis: financing approach × country/context × UHC outcome × evidence strength. Quantitative estimates remain in original metrics unless justified pooling is possible. Bibliometric/science-mapping analyses describe knowledge development and evidence concentration and do not substitute for study-level evidence.

REPORTING
Report according to PRISMA 2020. Document the exact OpenAlex search using PRISMA-S principles where applicable. Clearly distinguish retrieval corpus, unique screened records, full-text reports and final included studies.
